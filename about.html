Meet the Founder

Hi — I'm Charis

I've always been really sentimental about jewelry and tend to attach memories to the pieces I wear, so it was frustrating when I'd buy something I loved, only for it to rust or tarnish and eventually end up in the bin after a short time.

After experiencing that so many times, I thought, surely I'm not the only one who feels this way.

That's really where Areli came from. I wanted to create jewelry that you could actually keep and enjoy for a long time — not something that would itch or you'd have to replace every few months. That's why our pieces are made from quality stainless steel, so you can wear the pieces you love without constantly worrying about them losing their beauty.

Because the pieces that mean something to you deserve to last.

Confidence, Every Day

With our hypoallergenic, tarnish-free, and waterproof jewelry, we aim to enhance your confidence with our versatile designs that fit seamlessly into any lifestyle. Whether at work, the gym, or a night out, our jewelry stays beautiful, durable, and comfortable.
